Project Overview

Vantara Heritage is a prestigious 55+ acre gated mega-township situated at Brijghat, Garhmukteshwar. It blends the holy heritage of the Ganges with high-specification villa and farmhouse plots.

Offering world-class living just an hour's drive from Delhi NCR on NH-9, Vantara Heritage provides wide asphalt roads, private artificial lakes, grand entry gates, and resort-grade amenities.
