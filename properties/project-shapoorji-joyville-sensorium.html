Project Overview

Shapoorji Pallonji Joyville Sensorium in Hinjawadi Phase 1 is a 10.5-acre biophilic sanctuary with 75% open spaces, 2.8-acre sunken garden, and riverside green promenades.

Imbued with sensory reflexology paths, aroma gardens, infinity swimming pools, and smart app automation, it provides peace and vitality for modern IT families.
