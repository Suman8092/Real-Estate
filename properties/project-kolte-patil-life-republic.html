Project Overview

Life Republic by Kolte-Patil is a celebrated 390-acre integrated township in Hinjawadi, Pune. Housing thousands of happy families with operational schools, sports complexes, and nature lakes.

With internal 150-ft wide spine roads, functioning international schools, high-street shopping, and multiple clubhouses, Life Republic is a self-sustaining city within a city.
