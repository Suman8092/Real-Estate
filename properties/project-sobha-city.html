Project Overview

Sobha City in Sector 108 Gurugram spans 39 contiguous acres right next to the Delhi border on the Dwarka Expressway. It features 8.5 acres of continuous urban park, two 40,000 sq. ft. clubhouses, and a half-acre lakelet.

Constructed with Sobha's industry-reputed in-house backward integration model, Sobha City offers Olympic-size swimming pools, full cricket grounds, and exceptional German-finish build quality.
