Project Overview

Fairfox Eye of Noida (EON) is an iconic 13.5-acre IGBC Gold certified commercial development situated at Sector 140A Noida Expressway. It sets a new benchmark in sustainable corporate workspaces and lifestyle retail.

Featuring pedestrian-friendly multi-level skywalks, expansive central green courtyards, international dining zones, and high-tech lockable workspaces, EON is the epicenter of business along the Expressway.
