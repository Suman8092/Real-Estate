Project Overview

Lifestyle Central by BNX Group is Greater Noida's marquee commercial and hospitality landmark located in Delta 2. It integrates high-street luxury retail, serviced hotel suites, banquet facilities, and a panoramic rooftop leisure club.

Rising 18 storeys high, Lifestyle Central redefines urban leisure and business. With lower levels catering to high-end global retail brands and dining, the upper floors feature 133 fully serviced boutique suites operated under global hospitality standards.
