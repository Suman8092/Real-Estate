Project Overview

Anantara is an expansive 23.25-acre AgriForest community located adjacent to the Kalagarh Dam and Amangarh wildlife corridor. It integrates sustainable agro-forestry with eco-cottages.

Anantara offers an authentic country living experience where every plot owner benefits from shared fruit plantations, medicinal flora, and professionally maintained sustainable farming practices.
