Project Overview

Elan The Presidential in Sector 106 Gurugram is spread over 30 contiguous acres right on the Dwarka Expressway. It boasts an 8-hole golf putting green, horse riding track, and 3 lavish clubhouses.

Featuring futuristic aerodynamic tower designs, Olympic lap pools, dedicated butler service, and crystal lagoons, it sets a global gold standard in luxury living.
