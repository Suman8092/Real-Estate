Project Overview

Godrej Tropical Isle in Sector 44 Noida brings ultra-luxury island resort-style living to the heart of Noida. Featuring island-inspired architecture, floating cabanas, private decks, and air purification systems.

Strategically positioned at Sector 44 right at the start of the Noida-Greater Noida Expressway, Godrej Tropical Isle offers seamless access to South Delhi and Central Noida while enveloping residents in lush tropical greenery.
